# Royal Logics

Build a modern, high-converting landing page for a TV repair business. The design should be clean and professional.




Use this EXACT color palette (Tailwind classes/Hex):




Primary Brand Color (Header, Footer, Icons): Deep Navy Blue (#0F172A / bg-slate-900)

Accent/Action Color (All Buttons, Links, Checkmarks): Bright Orange (#F97316 / bg-orange-500)

Background Color: Off-White/Light Gray for sections to create contrast (#F8FAFC / bg-slate-50)

Card Backgrounds: Pure White (#FFFFFF) with subtle shadow (shadow-md)

Text Colors: Dark Gray for headings (text-slate-900), Medium Gray for paragraphs (text-slate-600).

Use Tailwind CSS for styling and Shadcn UI components for interactivity. The page should be structured with the following sections in order:




1. Header / Navigation:




Background: Pure White (#FFFFFF), sticky at the top.

Left side: Text logo "TV Repair Specialists" (bold, text-slate-900).

Middle links: Home, Services, How It Works, Contact Us (text-slate-600 hover to text-orange-500).

Right side: A prominent "Call Now: [Your Phone Number]" button (bg-orange-500 text white).

2. Hero Section (Split Layout):




Background: Light Gray (bg-slate-50).

Left Column (Text):




Headline: "Expert TV Repairs — Fast, Reliable & Affordable" (text-slate-900).

Subheadline: "From cracked screens to no-picture problems, we fix all TV brands and types across [Your City]. Same-day service available."

Bullet points with Orange checkmark icons: "Same-day service", "All major brands", "6-month warranty on parts & labour", "Certified technicians with 10+ years experience".

A badge showing "★★★★★ 4.9/5 from 200+ happy customers".

Right Column (Lead Capture Form):




A card with a white background (bg-white) and shadow, titled "Book Your TV Repair".

Input fields: Full Name, Phone Number, Email Address.

Dropdown menu titled "TV Type / Problem" with options: Smart TV - No Internet, LED/LCD - Backlight issue, Power - Won't turn on, Sound issues, Screen replacement.

Textarea: "Describe the Problem".

Full-width submit button: "Send Message" (bg-orange-500).

3. Trust Bar (Full width, right below hero):




Background: Deep Navy Blue (bg-slate-900), Text: White.

4 columns with Orange icons and text: "Same-day service", "90-day warranty", "All major brands", "On-site across [Your City]".

4. Services Grid (3-column layout with cards):




Section Title: "Common TV Problems We Fix"

Create 6 white cards (bg-white), each with an Orange icon, a title, a short description, and a "Book a Technician" text link (text-orange-500):




Card 1: "TV Not Turning On" - "Usually caused by a faulty power supply board, damaged capacitors, or surge damage."

Card 2: "No Picture but Sound" - "Commonly caused by backlight failure, inverter issues, or a damaged display panel."

Card 3: "Lines or Shadows on Screen" - "Horizontal/vertical lines usually indicate a faulty T-Con board or loose cables."

Card 4: "Turns On and Off by Itself" - "Often caused by a failing power board or software firmware glitches."

Card 5: "HDMI Ports Not Working" - "We repair and replace damaged HDMI ports and motherboard connectors."

Card 6: "Smart TV Connectivity" - "Expert troubleshooting for Wi-Fi, app, or streaming connectivity issues."

5. How It Works Section (4-column step process):




Section Title: "Get Your TV Fixed in 4 Easy Steps"

Use large Orange numbers (1, 2, 3, 4) for each step.

Step 1: "Contact Us" (Call or fill out our form).

Step 2: "Free Diagnosis" (We assess your TV fast).

Step 3: "Get a Quote" (Transparent pricing with no hidden costs).

Step 4: "Enjoy Your TV" (Most repairs are done same day).

6. Statistics / Authority Section:




Background: Deep Navy Blue (bg-slate-900), Text: White.

4 large statistics: "10+ Years Experience", "4.9/5 Average Rating", "5,000+ TVs Repaired", "90-Day Warranty". (Numbers in Orange).

7. Supported Brands:




Background: Light Gray (bg-slate-50).

Section titled "Brands We Repair" listing: Samsung, LG, Sony, Hisense, TCL, Skyworth, Philips, Panasonic.

8. Footer:




Background: Deep Navy Blue (bg-slate-900), Text: Light Gray (text-slate-300).

Include copyright text, email address [Your Email], phone number [Your Phone Number], and a link back to the top.

Technical Requirements:




Ensure the design is fully responsive (stacks correctly on mobile).

The hero form should look functional.

Use modern, subtle hover effects on buttons and cards (e.g., hover state for Orange buttons should be slightly darker, like bg-orange-600).
